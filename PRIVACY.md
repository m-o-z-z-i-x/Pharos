# Privacy

Pharos has no server. The extension collects no statistics and sends nothing to the developer

**What is stored in your browser:** the list of connected services with the account names (an address or a @handle), settings, the latest notification counts, a week of check history, tokens and sessions of services with an official API. Everything stays in the browser profile and is not available to websites

**Where requests go:** only to the services you connected and to the public project repository, once a day, to update the rule catalog (no data of yours is sent)

**What the extension does not do:** it takes only numbers (and a sender name where a service gives it) out of the answers and never stores, shows or sends message texts, feeds or contacts, does not publish anything and does not send anything on your behalf

Everything can be removed in the settings: «Data and permissions» → «Erase everything». Access to any site can be revoked there too
